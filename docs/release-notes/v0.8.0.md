## Tibyan 0.8.0 (build 45)

- **Widgets in the app's look:** Verse of the Day, Today's portion, Tasbih and Shortcuts are drawn Geometric, Golden night or Manuscript, light or night, following the app by default.
- **A look per widget:** choose each widget's look and mode from its settings.
- **«Listen» on Verse of the Day:** recites that verse; Today's portion shows the plan's day and a progress bar.

**Downloads:** Android APKs, macOS, Windows and Linux. iOS & Apple Watch: [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/gxJ4rrdd). Verify files with `SHA256SUMS`.

> **Linux:** needs libmpv for recitation (`apt install libmpv2`). / يحتاج libmpv لتشغيل التلاوة.
