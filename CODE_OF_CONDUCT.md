# Code of Conduct · ميثاق السلوك

Tibyan serves the Quran. We expect everyone in this project to act with the manners that befit that: honesty, patience and respect.

يخدم تبيان القرآن الكريم، فنرجو من كل مشارك الصدق والصبر والاحترام.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique work, not people.
- Keep discussions on the project. Differences between schools of thought are respected; debates about them belong elsewhere.
- Credit others' work and sources.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or demeaning remarks about anyone's faith, school, ethnicity, gender or language.
- Mocking religious content or people.
- Sharing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **info@altibyan.app**. The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants who break this code. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
